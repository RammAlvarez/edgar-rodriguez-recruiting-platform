## 5. Decisions (continuing the log)

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D40 | **Precedence for numbers and rules:** SPEC / DATA_MODEL (and their decisions) → the client's Excel files → the prototype. The prototype (`Plataforma.dc.html`, `data.js`) is the reference for screens, flows and copy only; its formulas are not a source (e.g. it computes descanso laborado as `qty × SD`, the sheet and SPEC pay a fixed 500; it counts a festivo as a worked day). |
| D41 | **Golden test definition:** the test reads the workbook from `examples/` (ClosedXML in the test project; never transcribed by hand). Inputs per row: puesto rates from the row (`G`, `I`, `J`), días (`F`), Sunday worked (`M` present), partial hours and overtime hours (the multiplier in `N` / `P`), holiday entitlement (`O` present), descanso laborado (`R`). Expected per row: every column except `O` equals the Excel cached value within 1e-6 (Excel stores doubles; the engine is exact); `O` equals D14 (4 × costo/hora × 3); the exact row total equals Σ items; the payable equals `trunc(exact, 2)`. **Known diffs** (asserted, listed in the test with the sheet's value): 34 rows 428.50 → 428.52, 5 rows 585.70 → 585.60, rows 65, 83, 99, 117 249.50 → 428.52, rows 81, 85, 87 178.60 → 428.52 (Q12). Expected period: exact 334,655.149146 (items stored with 6 decimals, D42; 334,655.149166… before the cut), payable Σ 334,654.83 (the sheet's own inputs give 333,188.81 payable; Excel prints 333,189.13). |
| D42 | **Never round; store 6 decimals; truncate to cents only at payment** (owner, 2026-10-16; 6 decimals added at P6). Every item is calculated exactly (C# `decimal`) and stored truncated to 6 decimals (`numeric(18,6)`; 71.4283333… → 71.428333; the cut is applied once to each item's final amount, never to an intermediate that is reused); line sums are sums of those stored items. The payable `total_net = trunc(total_net_exact, 2)` — truncation, not rounding (1,234.5678 → 1,234.56) — applied once per worker, because each worker's net is the last calculation (one transfer per worker). The bank file rows, batch totals, `payroll_periods.total_net` and reports' payable totals are sums of those truncated amounts. Other grid/export columns display the stored values truncated to 2 dp (display only). User-typed money (rates, incidents, salaries) stays `numeric(12,2)`. Supersedes the kickoff's "AwayFromZero once per line item" and SPEC §5.5's "half-up at the line level". On SEM 38 the 6-decimal cut moves the exact period sum from 334,655.149166… to 334,655.149146 and changes no payable amount. |
| D43 | **FESTIVO PRIMERA MITAD = D14 for every entitled row** (owner, 2026-10-16: holidays are paid triple, 4 h × costo/hora × 3). The sheet's typed 428.5 / 585.7 are the same rule short by 2 cents / over by 10; 249.50 and 178.60 are listed as known diffs and asked of the client (SPEC Q12). |
